A known host that suddenly scans the rest of the subnet

192.168.0.1 is not an outsider — it is a legitimate host on this network, with 1,252 normal-labeled rows of its own (all TCP/websocket traffic with 192.168.0.111). During "address scan" the SAME host ARP-resolves and TCP-SYN-probes 5 hosts it has never contacted before, spread across almost the entire 99.4-minute session — not one short burst. Only in the last 0.075% of its footprint does it ever touch Modbus.
